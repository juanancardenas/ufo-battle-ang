Welcome to UFO Battle

Use the menu to navigate through the different options:

  • Preferences: Enter the number of UFO and the time of the play. Once you click on "Go!" the play will be started.
  • Play: Uses Left and Right Arrows to move the rocket and use Space to Trigger it. Enjoy the play!
  • Records: A list of the 10 best plays will be showed up.
  • Register: Create your own account for UFO Battle. (Under construction, only form is available yet)
  • Log-in: Log in into your account. (Under construction, only token fetch and error handling are available yet)
